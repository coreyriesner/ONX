About the data

Property lines and owners come from the MN DNR statewide parcel compilation and MnGeo's open county parcels, queried live. Coverage is uneven: all 87 counties have lines, but some counties don't publish owner names (see the coverage note). Always confirm ownership and get permission before hunting private land. This app is not a legal survey.
