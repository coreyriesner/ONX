# Northwoods Hunt Map

A hunting map for Minnesota that runs in the browser. It has:

- Property lines and landowner names for all 87 counties, from MN DNR and MnGeo county parcel data.
- Public land: DNR state land, WMAs, county tax-forfeit land, national forest, USFWS WPAs and refuges, and Walk-In Access. A legend lets you switch to a "Public only" view.
- Distance and area (acres) measuring, plus waypoints with hunting icons. Waypoints export to GPX and GeoJSON.
- Weather, wind, sunrise and sunset, legal shooting hours and moon phase.
- Installs to the phone home screen and keeps recently viewed areas for when you're offline.

Open https://coreyriesner.github.io/ONX/ on your phone, then use Share → Add to Home Screen.

Data is pulled live from public MN DNR, MnGeo, USFS, USFWS, Esri, USGS and Open-Meteo services. It isn't a legal survey, so confirm ownership and get permission before you hunt.
